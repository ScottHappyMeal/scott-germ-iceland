Reykjabod Hot Springs, Hveragerdi

Opened September 2026. A quieter mountain-valley soak right on the Dec 29 drive, about 15 minutes from the domes.

Winter hours: Monday to Thursday 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Friday to Sunday 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Leave the water 30 minutes before close. The restaurant is open 11:30 AM to 8:30 PM.

  • Classic 17,800 ISK (about $142) for two. Lagoon, outdoor sauna, cold plunge, Kneipp, and a towel.
  • Signature 33,800 ISK (about $270) for two. Adds the adults-only indoor Bathhouse Spa with 10 sensory rooms, bathrobes, slippers, and 1 drink each.

Reykjabod website

Sky Lagoon, Kopavogur

About 15 minutes from The Reykjavik EDITION. A larger, busier 70 m ocean-edge infinity lagoon with the 7-step Skjol ritual.

Winter hours (October 21 to April 30): 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily. On December 31: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Leave the water 30 minutes before close.

  • Saman from 29,980 ISK (about $240) for two. Floor price, which varies by time slot. Shared changing, the ritual, and a towel.
  • Ser from 35,980 ISK (about $288) for two. Floor price, which varies by time slot. Private changing cabin, plus lotion and a bag.
